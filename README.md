3.1 Information We Generally DO NOT Collect

For most of our Apps, we do NOT collect, store, or 
transmit any of the following personal information:
• Your name, email address, or phone number
• Your physical address or location data
• Your contacts, calendar, or SMS messages
• Your photos, videos, or media files
• Your browsing history
• Your device's unique identifiers (IMEI, MAC address)
• Your financial or payment information

3.2 Information Stored Locally On Your Device

Some of our Apps may store the following information 
ONLY on your own device, using secure local storage 
(such as SharedPreferences, SQLite, or similar):

• App preferences and settings
• Quiz scores, progress, and statistics
• Bookmarked or favorite content
• Offline content data for faster loading
• Game progress and achievements

This data NEVER leaves your device and is NEVER 
transmitted to TR Software Solution or any third party. 
It is automatically removed when you uninstall the App.

3.3 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Some of our Apps may use third-party services that 
collect certain information. These may include:

• Google Play Services (mandatory for Play Store apps)
• Google Play Billing (for in-app purchases, if any)
• Google Analytics for Firebase (only if enabled)
• Firebase Crashlytics (to fix crashes, only if enabled)
• AdMob or other ad networks (only if ads are present)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We 
encourage you to review them:

• Google Privacy Policy: 
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
• Firebase Privacy: 
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
• AdMob Privacy: 
  https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
